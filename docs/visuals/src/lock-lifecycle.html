Locking a tip

Optional · fan picks the duration · max 5 years

Lifecycle of a locked tip

Four transactions over the life of the lock. The cliff is set and checked against the chain clock, not the browser's.

1 · Tip locked

Fan signs one tx: swap + createVestingEscrowV2. Fan pays ~0.0051 SOL of escrow rent.

2 · Cliff

The chain clock passes the cliff. Before that the app disables Claim (the program would move 0).

3 · Creator claims

claimV2 from the jar. The T-Token lands in the creator's wallet, net of the 0.2% fee.

4 · Fan reclaims deposit

closeVestingEscrow after the full claim: the ~0.0051 SOL rent goes back to the fan.

The risk: Tessera's redemption window

T-Tokens are redeemed only during a 90-day window after a liquidity event (Tessera Terms 2.2). A lock can't be ended early.

Nobody knows when a liquidity event will happen, so any lock, short or long, could end up spanning the whole window. That's why the app shows the warning whenever a lock is selected, not only for long ones, and the Blink repeats it in its message.
